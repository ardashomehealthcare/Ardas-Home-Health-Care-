Privacy Policy

ARDAS Home Health Care ("ARDAS", "we", "us", "our") provides home healthcare services in Bathinda, Punjab — elderly care, home nursing, patient attendants, post-surgical care, physiotherapy, babysitter services and lab test facility at home. This policy explains what information we collect from patients, families, website visitors and job applicants, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Summary

In short: we collect only the details needed to arrange safe care at your home — contact details, patient condition and address. Health information stays confidential and is shared only with the professionals assigned to your care. We never sell your personal data. You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by calling or WhatsApp on +91 98770 62683.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you give us directly

  • Contact details — your name, phone number, WhatsApp number and, where shared, email address when you call, message or write to us.
  • Service enquiry details — the type of care required (caregiver, nurse, attendant, physiotherapist, babysitter or lab test), preferred shift timings and service location/address in Bathinda.
  • Patient information — age, gender, medical condition, mobility status, ongoing treatment and doctor's instructions, shared by the patient or family so we can plan safe care. This is sensitive personal information and is treated with strict confidentiality.
  • Job applicant information — for candidates applying through our careers page or WhatsApp: name, contact details, qualification certificates (e.g. GNM, ANM, B.Sc Nursing), identity documents such as Aadhaar card, address details and references, used for verification and placement.
  • Payment information — records of payments made by cash, UPI or bank transfer. We do not store card numbers or UPI PINs.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Website usage data — this website uses Google Analytics and Google Ads measurement (via Google tag). These tools may collect your approximate location, device/browser type, pages visited and visit duration through cookies or similar technologies. This data is aggregated and does not directly identify you.
  • Call & message metadata — the time and nature of your call or WhatsApp message, used to respond and maintain service records.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only for legitimate purposes connected with our home healthcare services:

  • To understand your requirement and recommend the right type of care and care plan.
  • To match, brief and assign a suitable, background-verified caregiver, nurse, attendant, physiotherapist, babysitter or phlebotomist to your home.
  • To share a transparent price quote, confirm bookings and coordinate shifts, replacements and schedule changes.
  • To monitor ongoing care quality, keep families updated about the patient's care, and handle feedback or complaints.
  • To process job applications — including identity verification, background checks and qualification screening of candidates.
  • To issue invoices, record payments and maintain accounts as required by law.
  • To improve our services, website and communication — including through aggregated analytics.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory or law-enforcement requirements, where applicable.

4. Health & Medical Information Confidentiality

Details about a patient's health, diagnosis, medication or daily condition are treated as highly confidential:

  • Health information is accessed only by the coordinators and professionals directly involved in that patient's care, on a strict need-to-know basis.
  • Our caregivers, nurses and attendants are instructed and expected to keep patient and family information private and to not photograph, record or share patient details on personal phones or social media.
  • We do not publish or use identifiable patient stories, photos or testimonials without the family's clear consent.

5. Phone Calls, WhatsApp & Messaging

Most of our coordination happens over phone and WhatsApp. When you contact us on these channels:

  • Your number and messages are processed by the telecom/WhatsApp platforms under their own privacy policies.
  • We use your number to respond to your enquiry, coordinate care, share quotes and updates, and — for staff — to share duty/job alerts in coordinator-run WhatsApp groups.
  • We do not add you to promotional broadcast lists unrelated to your enquiry or employment without your consent. You can ask us to stop messages at any time.

6. Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website uses limited cookies and similar technologies:

  • Google Analytics (gtag) — helps us understand how visitors find and use the site so we can improve it.
  • Google Ads measurement — helps us measure the effectiveness of our advertising.

These cookies do not give us your name or contact details. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings; the website will still work if you do. We do not run third-party tracking beyond the Google services listed above.

7. When We Share Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only in these situations:

  • Care professionals assigned to you — the caregiver, nurse, attendant, physiotherapist, babysitter or phlebotomist serving your home receives the contact, address and care details needed for the assignment.
  • Diagnostic/lab partners — only where you ask us to arrange a lab test, the details needed for sample collection and reporting are shared with the concerned lab.
  • Verification of job applicants — candidate identity and background details may be checked with issuing authorities, references or verification services, with the candidate's knowledge.
  • Legal obligations — where disclosure is required by law, court order or government/health authority.

8. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information: access is limited to authorised coordinators; physical documents are stored securely; and digital records are kept on access-controlled devices. Staff are bound to confidentiality as a condition of engagement. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — if you ever suspect misuse of your information, contact us immediately.

9. How Long We Keep Your Information

  • Enquiry details — kept for as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable follow-up period.
  • Service & patient records — kept while care continues and afterwards as needed for accounts, dispute resolution and legal compliance.
  • Job applications — kept while a candidate is engaged with us or being considered; unsuccessful applications are removed after a reasonable period unless the candidate asks us to keep them on file.

When information is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or destroyed.

10. Your Rights & Choices

You can, at any time and free of charge, ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your information, where it is no longer needed or where the law allows.
  • Withdraw consent for optional communication, such as updates not related to an ongoing service.

To exercise any of these, call or WhatsApp +91 98770 62683 or email info@ardashomehealthcare.com. We respond to genuine requests promptly.

11. Children's Information

Our babysitter services involve care of children, but we collect information about a child (age, routine, health needs) only from the parent or lawful guardian, and only to provide safe care. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children, online or otherwise.

12. Third-Party Websites

Our site may contain links to third-party platforms such as WhatsApp, Google Maps or phone/email handlers. Once you leave our website, their own privacy policies apply and we are not responsible for their practices.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or in the law. The latest version will always be posted on this page with its "Last updated" date. Continued use of our services after an update means the revised policy has been noted.
