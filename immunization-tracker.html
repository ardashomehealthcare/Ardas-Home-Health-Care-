📜 Govt. of India Instructions — UIP Protocol

This prototype draws on published UIP materials but has not been validated against all current government guidance. Always confirm with the Medical Officer / ANM at your Govt. facility.

Schedule shown is an illustrative national routine schedule, not a verified current or historical guideline. JE depends on district, historical rollout differs, and Vitamin A is a supplement, not a vaccine. Dates and windows require clinical validation; a health worker must plan catch-up based on actual records and applicable guidance.

Consult the NHM immunization operational guidelines and the NHM health worker FAQ; these documents may not reflect later updates. Check current national and state directions with your government health facility.

Add a calendar reminder

The downloaded .ics file contains an all-day event with a requested reminder one day before. Calendar apps may handle alerts differently; check the alert after importing. The event uses a generic title and does not include the child’s name or vaccination details.

Android

  1. Tap Download calendar file.
  2. Open the download and choose a calendar app that supports importing .ics files. If no app offers to import it, use a calendar service’s import feature or another calendar app.
  3. Check the date and alert, then save. Enable notifications for your calendar app.

iPhone

  1. Tap Download calendar file, then open it from Downloads or the Files app.
  2. Tap the event and choose Add to Calendar or Add All; choose a calendar if asked.
  3. Check the date and alert, then save. Enable Calendar notifications in Settings.

Steps vary by phone and app. Downloading alone does not save the event. Calendar alerts depend on the phone’s settings. This routine date is not a personalised catch-up recommendation; confirm it with a health worker.

Review the immunization record

Use the MCP card or another vaccination record. Enter the actual date for each dose recorded as given. Enter the actual date if given, or select “Missed / not given” only if you know it was not given. Leave both blank if unsure. Blank does not mean missed.

Important: This prototype cannot prescribe catch-up. A health worker must review previous doses, intervals, local guidance and eligibility.
