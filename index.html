Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking a caregiver

Is ARDAS HOME HEALTH CARE a registered home healthcare agency?

Yes, ARDAS HOME HEALTH CARE is a registered healthcare service provider

How quickly can ARDAS HOME HEALTH CARE provide a caregiver at home?

Most requirements are fulfilled within 24 hours. For urgent cases, we can arrange a caregiver within 4-6 hours.

How does ARDAS HOME HEALTH CARE monitor caregivers after deployment?

We use a technology-enabled system including daily attendance via mobile app, real-time updates for families, supervisor visits, 24/7 helpline for support, and weekly feedback collection. This ensures consistent and high-quality care.

What happens if the assigned caregiver is not suitable?

ARDAS HOME HEALTH CARE offers hassle-free caregiver replacement. You can request a change anytime and we typically provide an alternative within 24 hours.

How does ARDAS HOME HEALTH CARE ensure safety of caregivers sent to homes?

All caregivers undergo personality screening, Aadhaar/Background verification, medical fitness testing, hands-on training, and continuous supervision. Families receive identity details before deployment, and real-time attendance is recorded through our mobile app.
