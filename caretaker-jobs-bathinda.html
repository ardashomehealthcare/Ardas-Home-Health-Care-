02 — ATTENDANT

Personal hygiene, diaper & toilet care, feeding, turning/repositioning, mobility and patient sitting — at home or during hospital admission.

Feeding as directed, diapering, hygiene, safe play, nap & night care — trained and background-verified, with parent supervision.

Questions from Job Seekers

Before you apply — quickly checked

Is there any fee to apply or to get duty?

No. The Application Form is completely free. You never pay to apply, to be verified or to join the WhatsApp group. Report anyone asking for money at +91 98770 62683.

How will I know if I’m selected?

Our coordinator reviews your form (name, documents, duty preference, gender) and contacts suitable candidates on the phone/WhatsApp you gave. Joining the WhatsApp group after submit ensures you see new openings instantly.

Do you give duties near my home?

We try to match your area — Model Town, Ajit Road, Mall Road, Power House Road, Thermal Colony, Bibi Wala etc. — and nearby towns (Rampura, Maur, Talwandi Sabo…). Mention your locality clearly in the address field.

What if I only want Day or only Night duty?

Tick only that box in the form. You can tick multiple too (Day + Night + 24 Hrs) for more chances.

I’m already working elsewhere — can I still apply?

Yes. Many caretakers apply while working. We keep your info confidential and only contact you for duties that match your preference.

What happens after I submit the form?

You’ll see a “Join WhatsApp Group” card on the form itself — Male and Female have separate links. Tap it to join. You’ll also be contacted for verification / next steps.

Can I apply offline or by call?

The fastest way is the online form (2 mins). You can also call +91 98770 62683 Mon–Sat 9AM–5PM and we’ll guide you.
