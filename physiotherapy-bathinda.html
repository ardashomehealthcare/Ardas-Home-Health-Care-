Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, answered

How long is each physiotherapy session, and how many will I need?

Sessions typically last 30–45 minutes. The number of sessions depends on the condition and recovery goals — the physiotherapist shares a plan after the first assessment.

Does the physiotherapist bring equipment?

Therapists carry the essential items needed for the session and also use what is safely available at home. Any additional equipment is suggested to the family in advance.

How soon after surgery can physiotherapy start at home?

Usually within days of the surgeon's clearance. Always follow your operating doctor's advice; ARDAS can start home sessions as soon as the doctor permits.

What is the charge for physiotherapy at home in Bathinda?

Charges depend on the condition, session length and number of visits. Call or WhatsApp +91 98770 62683 for current per-session and package rates.

Can physiotherapy be combined with a caregiver or nurse?

Yes. Many families combine physiotherapy sessions with an ARDAS attendant or nurse for daily care between sessions.
