Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, answered

When should I book post-surgical home care — before or after the surgery?

The best time is before discharge. Share the expected surgery or discharge date and ARDAS will line up a caregiver so that support starts the day the patient reaches home.

How long is post-surgical care usually needed?

It depends on the surgery and recovery speed — commonly a few weeks. Service can continue, reduce or stop as the patient recovers; you decide with our coordinator.

Does home care replace hospital nursing?

No. ARDAS home care supports recovery at home; any clinical procedure is performed per your treating doctor's instructions, and a qualified nurse can be added when prescribed.

Can I combine a nurse, an attendant and a physiotherapist?

Yes. Many recovery plans combine an attendant for daily care, a nurse for procedures and home physiotherapy sessions. ARDAS coordinates all of them together.

What does post-surgical care at home cost in Bathinda?
